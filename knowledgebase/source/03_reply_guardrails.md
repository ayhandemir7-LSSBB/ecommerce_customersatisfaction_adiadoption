# Spreekorb Reply Guardrails

Version 1.0 · Owners: Data Protection Officer, Legal, Customer Experience
*Invented for an Ironhack case study. Spreekorb is not a real company. Not legal advice.*

Every reply, written by a person or drafted by AI, must pass these rules. An AI draft that breaks a rule is rejected or edited by the agent.

## 1. Data protection (GDPR)
- Never ask for or repeat full card numbers, IBANs, passwords, PINs or ID document numbers.
- Do not repeat personal data that the reply does not need (address, phone, date of birth).
- Do not ask for or comment on health information.
- Access, deletion or correction requests: do not answer the content. Reply that the Data Protection Officer will handle it and forward the email to datenschutz@spreekorb.example.
- Do not mention other customers or their orders.

## 2. AI use (EU AI Act and company rules)
- Drafts are suggestions. A person reviews every reply before it is sent.
- Never write "as an AI", "I am an AI model" or similar inside a reply. The agent who sends the reply is responsible for it.
- Never claim a check, a call or an action that has not happened ("I have checked with the warehouse").

## 3. Accuracy and liability
- Use only facts from the customer's email or the Customer Service Policy. If a fact is missing, write a placeholder: [order number], [date], [amount].
- No guarantees: never "we guarantee", "100%", "definitely", "for sure".
- No promises of refunds, replacements, vouchers or compensation unless the policy allows them for exactly this case.
- No admission of legal fault, no legal advice, no blaming the customer, the courier or a colleague.

## 4. Banned phrases (AI slop and bad service)
Do not use these phrases or close variations:

| English | German |
|---|---|
| I hope this email finds you well | Ich hoffe, diese E-Mail erreicht dich gut |
| Rest assured | Seien Sie versichert |
| We apologize for any inconvenience caused | Wir entschuldigen uns für etwaige Unannehmlichkeiten |
| Thank you for reaching out to us | Vielen Dank, dass Sie sich an uns gewandt haben |
| Seamless, delve, leverage, elevate | nahtlos, maßgeschneidert, ganzheitlich |
| As I already said / As mentioned before | Wie bereits erwähnt |
| Unfortunately that is not possible (without an alternative) | Leider nicht möglich (ohne Alternative) |
| Your fault / you should have | Ihr Fehler / Sie hätten |
| We guarantee / 100% | Wir garantieren / 100 % |

**Style bans:** no em dashes, no exclamation marks in apologies, no more than one apology per reply, no emojis.

## 5. When in doubt
Do not send. Set the email to "Needs team lead" and add a one-line note.
