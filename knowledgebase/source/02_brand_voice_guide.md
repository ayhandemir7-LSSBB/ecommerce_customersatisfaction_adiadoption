# Spreekorb Brand Voice Guide

Version 1.0 · Owner: Customer Experience
*Invented for an Ironhack case study. Spreekorb is not a real company.*

## Page 1: How Spreekorb sounds

**In one line:** like a helpful neighbour from Berlin who knows the shop well: friendly, direct, honest.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Say the answer first, then the details | Start with long apologies or thanks |
| Short sentences, plain words | Jargon, legal wording, marketing hype |
| German: "du", warm but not childish | German: "Sie" (except in legal or complaint escalations) |
| English: friendly and simple | English: corporate phrases |
| Name the next step and when it happens | Vague phrases like "soon" or "as quickly as possible" |
| Say what we can do when we cannot do something | A plain "no" without an alternative |
| One apology when we made a mistake | Apologising in every sentence |

**Structure of every reply**
1. Greeting with the first name if known: "Hallo [Vorname]," / "Hi [first name],"
2. The answer or the next step in one or two sentences.
3. Details the customer needs (what they do, what we do, by when).
4. One closing line offering help.
5. Sign-off: "Viele Grüße, dein Spreekorb Kundenservice" / "Best wishes, Spreekorb Customer Service"

**Length:** 60 to 120 words. Bullets only for steps the customer must follow.

## Page 2: Example replies

**Example 1 (German, return question)**
> Hallo Lena,
> du kannst deine Jacke innerhalb von 30 Tagen nach Lieferung kostenlos zurückschicken. So geht's:
> 1. In deinem Konto unter Mein Spreekorb > Bestellungen > Rücksendung das Label erstellen.
> 2. Paket bei einer DHL-Filiale abgeben.
> Sobald die Jacke bei uns geprüft ist, bekommst du dein Geld auf dem gleichen Zahlungsweg zurück.
> Viele Grüße, dein Spreekorb Kundenservice

**Example 2 (German, delivery delay)**
> Hallo Murat,
> danke für deine Nachricht. Deine Sendung zeigt seit einigen Tagen kein Update. Wir haben deshalb einen Nachforschungsauftrag bei DHL gestartet und melden uns spätestens in 3 Werktagen mit einem Ergebnis bei dir.
> Viele Grüße, dein Spreekorb Kundenservice

**Example 3 (English, refund status)**
> Hi Sarah,
> your return reached our warehouse and is being checked now. Once the check is done, the refund goes back to your PayPal account, usually within 1 to 2 working days.
> If you have not received it by [date], just reply to this email.
> Best wishes, Spreekorb Customer Service

**Example 4 (English, damaged item)**
> Hi Tom,
> sorry that your lamp arrived damaged. Could you send us two photos, one of the lamp and one of the box? Then we can offer you a replacement or a refund straight away.
> Best wishes, Spreekorb Customer Service

*Names in the examples are invented.*
