# Spreekorb Customer Service Policy (Kundenservice-Richtlinie)

Version 1.0 · Valid from 1 Nov 2026 · Owner: Head of Customer Service
*Invented for an Ironhack case study. Spreekorb is not a real company. Not legal advice.*

## 1. Purpose
This policy tells every customer service agent what Spreekorb promises its customers. Replies must never promise more than this policy allows. When a case is not covered, the agent asks the team lead before replying.

## 2. Orders and delivery
- Delivery within Germany: 1 to 3 working days after dispatch with DHL. Austria and the Netherlands: 3 to 5 working days.
- Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day.
- Shipping is free from 49 EUR order value in Germany. Below that, shipping costs 4.95 EUR.
- Every order confirmation and dispatch email contains the order number and a tracking link.
- If a parcel shows no tracking update for 5 working days, the agent opens a DHL investigation and tells the customer we will update them within 3 working days.

## 3. Cancellation
- An order can be cancelled free of charge until it is dispatched. The agent checks the order status before confirming.
- After dispatch, the customer refuses the parcel or returns it under section 4.

## 4. Returns and withdrawal
- Statutory right of withdrawal: 14 days from delivery, without giving a reason.
- Spreekorb goodwill return window: 30 days from delivery for unused items in original packaging.
- Returns within Germany are free with the prepaid label from the customer account (Mein Spreekorb > Bestellungen > Rücksendung).
- Excluded from returns: opened hygiene products, personalised items, gift cards.

## 5. Refunds
- Refunds go to the original payment method.
- Spreekorb refunds within 14 days of the withdrawal and may wait until the returned item has arrived at the warehouse.
- Typical timing after the warehouse check: PayPal 1 to 2 working days, card 3 to 5 working days, invoice (Klarna) per the provider's process.
- Agents never confirm a refund amount before the warehouse check is complete.

## 6. Damaged, faulty or wrong items
- Statutory warranty (Gewährleistung): 2 years from delivery.
- The customer sends photos of the item and the packaging. The agent then offers repair, replacement or refund, as the team lead approves.
- Wrong item delivered: Spreekorb sends a free return label and ships the correct item once it is in stock.

## 7. Payments and invoices
- Payment methods: PayPal, credit card, Klarna invoice, Apple Pay.
- Invoices are in the customer account as PDF. Agents can resend an invoice; they never change an invoice amount.
- Payment disputes and chargebacks go to the Billing team.

## 8. Email service levels
- Target: first response within 24 hours for 90% of emails, 7 days a week.
- Priority: critical within 4 hours (safety, legal threat, data protection request), high within 12 hours (damaged item, missing refund past the stated time), others within 24 hours.

## 9. Escalation (agent must not handle alone)
| Case | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Data protection request (access, deletion, correction) | Data Protection Officer, datenschutz@spreekorb.example |
| Legal threat or lawyer letter | Legal team |
| Injury or product safety risk | Team lead immediately (critical) |
| Payment dispute, chargeback, fraud suspicion | Billing team |
| Press or social media escalation | Communications team |

## 10. Use of AI drafts
Agents may use the AI draft assistant. The agent reads, edits and decides on every reply. A draft is never sent without an agent's approval. Facts in a reply must come from this policy or from the customer's email.
