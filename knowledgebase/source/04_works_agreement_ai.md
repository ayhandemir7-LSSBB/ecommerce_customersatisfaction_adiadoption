# Works Agreement on AI in Customer Service (draft)
# Betriebsvereinbarung KI im Kundenservice (Entwurf)

Between Spreekorb GmbH management and the Spreekorb works council · Draft 0.3 · For negotiation
*Invented for an Ironhack case study. Spreekorb is not a real company. Not legal advice.*

## 1. Scope
This agreement covers the AI email draft assistant in the customer service team (email channel). Any other AI tool needs its own agreement.

## 2. Purpose
The assistant sorts incoming emails and drafts replies, so that customers get faster answers and agents spend less time on routine emails. It is not used to assess, rank or monitor employees.

## 3. Human decision
- Every reply is approved, edited or rejected by an agent. Nothing is sent automatically.
- Agents may ignore or reject any draft without giving a reason.
- The agent stays responsible for the reply; the assistant is a tool.

## 4. No performance monitoring
- No individual statistics: no per-agent counts of approved, edited or rejected drafts, no speed or quality scores per person.
- Reports show team level only, with a minimum group size of 5 agents.
- Data from the assistant is never used for appraisals, pay decisions, warnings or dismissals.
- Basis: co-determination under § 87(1) No. 6 BetrVG; the works council was informed at the planning stage (§ 90 BetrVG).

## 5. Data and logging
- Logged per email: queue, priority, AI draft, final reply, status, time stamps. Agent names or IDs are not stored with these logs.
- Logs are kept for 90 days for quality checks, then deleted or anonymised.
- Customer personal data is handled under the Reply Guardrails and the data processing agreement with the AI provider.

## 6. Training and support
- Every agent gets training before using the assistant (AI literacy, EU AI Act Article 4): what it does, its limits, how to spot wrong drafts.
- Using the assistant is voluntary during the pilot.

## 7. Pilot and review
- Pilot: 8 weeks, one team, opt-in.
- Management and works council review the results after the pilot and then every 6 months: customer outcomes, agent feedback (anonymous), errors found.
- Changes to the assistant's purpose or data need the works council's agreement first.
