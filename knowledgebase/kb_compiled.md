=== 01_customer_service_policy ===
Spreekorb Customer Service Policy (Kundenservice-Richtlinie)
Version 1.0 · Valid from 1 Nov 2026 · Owner: Head of Customer Service
1. Purpose
This policy tells every customer service agent what Spreekorb promises its customers. Replies must never
promise more than this policy allows. When a case is not covered, the agent asks the team lead before
replying.
2. Orders and delivery
- Delivery within Germany: 1 to 3 working days after dispatch with DHL. Austria and the Netherlands: 3 to 5
working days.
- Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day.
- Shipping is free from 49 EUR order value in Germany. Below that, shipping costs 4.95 EUR.
- Every order confirmation and dispatch email contains the order number and a tracking link.
- If a parcel shows no tracking update for 5 working days, the agent opens a DHL investigation and tells the
customer we will update them within 3 working days.
3. Cancellation
- An order can be cancelled free of charge until it is dispatched. The agent checks the order status before
confirming.
- After dispatch, the customer refuses the parcel or returns it under section 4.
4. Returns and withdrawal
- Statutory right of withdrawal: 14 days from delivery, without giving a reason.
- Spreekorb goodwill return window: 30 days from delivery for unused items in original packaging.
- Returns within Germany are free with the prepaid label from the customer account (Mein Spreekorb >
Bestellungen > Rücksendung).
- Excluded from returns: opened hygiene products, personalised items, gift cards.
5. Refunds
- Refunds go to the original payment method.
- Spreekorb refunds within 14 days of the withdrawal and may wait until the returned item has arrived at the
warehouse.
- Typical timing after the warehouse check: PayPal 1 to 2 working days, card 3 to 5 working days, invoice
(Klarna) per the provider's process.
- Agents never confirm a refund amount before the warehouse check is complete.
6. Damaged, faulty or wrong items
- Statutory warranty (Gewährleistung): 2 years from delivery.
- The customer sends photos of the item and the packaging. The agent then offers repair, replacement or
refund, as the team lead approves.
- Wrong item delivered: Spreekorb sends a free return label and ships the correct item once it is in stock.
7. Payments and invoices
- Payment methods: PayPal, credit card, Klarna invoice, Apple Pay.
- Invoices are in the customer account as PDF. Agents can resend an invoice; they never change an invoice
amount.
- Payment disputes and chargebacks go to the Billing team.
8. Email service levels
- Target: first response within 24 hours for 90% of emails, 7 days a week.
- Priority: critical within 4 hours (safety, legal threat, data protection request), high within 12 hours (damaged
item, missing refund past the stated time), others within 24 hours.
9. Escalation (agent must not handle alone)

10. Use of AI drafts
Agents may use the AI draft assistant. The agent reads, edits and decides on every reply. A draft is never
sent without an agent's approval. Facts in a reply must come from this policy or from the customer's email.

=== 03_reply_guardrails ===
Spreekorb Reply Guardrails
Version 1.0 · Owners: Data Protection Officer, Legal, Customer Experience
Every reply, written by a person or drafted by AI, must pass these rules. An AI draft that breaks a rule is
rejected or edited by the agent.
1. Data protection (GDPR)
- Never ask for or repeat full card numbers, IBANs, passwords, PINs or ID document numbers.
- Do not repeat personal data that the reply does not need (address, phone, date of birth).
- Do not ask for or comment on health information.
- Access, deletion or correction requests: do not answer the content. Reply that the Data Protection Officer
will handle it and forward the email to datenschutz@spreekorb.example.
- Do not mention other customers or their orders.
2. AI use (EU AI Act and company rules)
- Drafts are suggestions. A person reviews every reply before it is sent.
- Never write "as an AI", "I am an AI model" or similar inside a reply. The agent who sends the reply is
responsible for it.
- Never claim a check, a call or an action that has not happened ("I have checked with the warehouse").
3. Accuracy and liability
- Use only facts from the customer's email or the Customer Service Policy. If a fact is missing, write a
placeholder: [order number], [date], [amount].
- No guarantees: never "we guarantee", "100%", "definitely", "for sure".
- No promises of refunds, replacements, vouchers or compensation unless the policy allows them for
exactly this case.
- No admission of legal fault, no legal advice, no blaming the customer, the courier or a colleague.

Style bans: no em dashes, no exclamation marks in apologies, no more than one apology per reply, no
emojis.
5. When in doubt
Do not send. Set the email to "Needs team lead" and add a one-line note.
