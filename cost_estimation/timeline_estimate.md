# Timeline estimate (R1-06)

Durations and costs are estimates (see the assumptions table in cost_analysis.md). No calendar dates are committed; weeks are counted from the stated start. Cost = days x day rate.

## Prototype

- Start: Done in Round 1
- Duration (estimate): about 2 weeks (done); effort 10 days
- Scope: n8n proof of concept, LangSmith monitoring, Tableau dashboard, synthetic data only.
- Exit criteria: Triage + draft run end to end; held-out check and timing sample done; human approves every draft.
- Owner (role): AI consultant
- Cost (estimate): 10 days x 760 EUR = 7,600.00 EUR (sunk, done in Round 1)

## Pilot

- Start: Starts after the Round 1 decision (week 1)
- Duration (estimate): about 6 weeks; effort 15 days
- Scope: Real-email test on 1 team: n8n + mailbox + review UI, DPA and works-council preparation.
- Exit criteria: DPA + EU region + works agreement signed; timing re-measured per email with several agents and counterbalanced order; CSAT and SLA tracked at team level (minimum group 5).
- Owner (role): Project lead with data protection officer and works council
- Cost (estimate): 15 days x 760 EUR = 11,400.00 EUR

## Rollout

- Start: Starts after the pilot exit criteria are met
- Duration (estimate): about 8 weeks; effort 10 days
- Scope: All email teams; hosting decision (n8n Cloud or self-hosted); Tableau Cloud licence if real data is shown.
- Exit criteria: Pilot results reviewed by management; team-level SLA and CSAT reported; support handover done.
- Owner (role): Head of customer service with IT owner
- Cost (estimate): 10 days x 760 EUR = 7,600.00 EUR
