# R1-01 Research pack (rubric: 20 pts with use cases)

**Outputs:** research/sector_research.md, research/opportunities_risks.md, research/use_cases.md

**Acceptance (each prints PASS/FAIL):**
- Sector, size (500 = large), why size matters
- Problems with numbers, each cited or marked estimate
- Data described in 2-3 sentences: what, where from, why it fits
- Opportunity/risk table + one risk avoided on purpose
- 2-3 use cases with why they fit a large company

**Checks:** reviewer, data check

**Out of scope:** anything not listed above.
